📊 Business Insights for Task 1
🔍 Key Insights
📈 Revenue Growth Trend

The business experienced consistent sales growth from 2014 to 2017, with 2017 generating the highest revenue. This indicates increasing customer demand and overall business expansion over time.

🛒 Category Performance

Technology emerged as the highest-performing category, contributing the largest share of total sales revenue. Office Supplies and Furniture followed behind, showing that technology products are the company’s strongest revenue driver.

🏆 Top-Selling Products

A small number of products generated a significant portion of total sales. Products such as Canon imageCLASS and Fellowes PB500 contributed strongly to revenue, highlighting the importance of high-performing inventory items.

🌍 Regional Analysis

The West region contributed the highest percentage of sales performance, while the South region generated the lowest share. This suggests stronger market penetration and customer demand in the West compared to other regions.

💰 Profitability Overview

The company generated over:

Total Sales: $2.29M
Total Profit: $286K
Total Quantity Sold: 37,873 units

This indicates healthy business performance and operational scalability.

💡 Business Recommendations
Increase investment in Technology products since they generate the highest revenue.
Focus marketing campaigns on top-selling products to maximize profitability.
Investigate low-performing regions such as the South to identify growth opportunities.
Analyze customer purchasing behavior to improve inventory planning and demand forecasting.
Expand successful strategies used in the West region into other markets.

This project analyzes Superstore sales data to identify business performance trends, top-performing products, regional sales distribution, and category profitability using Microsoft Excel.

The objective of this analysis is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can support strategic business decisions.

Identify top-selling products

Generate actionable business insights
